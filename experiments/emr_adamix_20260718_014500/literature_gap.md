# Literature Gap

V1 is project-specific unification, not a copied external module. The identified implementation gap is between:

- historical AdaMix: global mean-confidence adaptive patch sizing only; and
- historical EEMS: independent mixed-output entropy + symmetric KL with hard gates.

EMR-AdaMix instead transports detached source entropy-margin reliability through the exact AdaMix mask and weights only pseudo-source mixed supervision. No paper identity or novelty claim is registered here before a dedicated literature audit; status remains `PROJECT_SPECIFIC_DESIGN_GAP_SUPPORTED`.
